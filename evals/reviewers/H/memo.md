# Depot reliability review (synthetic benchmark H)

[P01] ## Executive summary

[P02] The depot completed 1,284 work orders in the review year, of which 38.6% were corrective. Availability of the fleet was 96.2% against a target of 97.0%. Three assets account for 41% of corrective hours. We recommend a condition-monitoring trial on the traction converters, a review of the brake pad change interval, and a stores audit. This summary is 58 words.

[P03] ## Scope and data

[P04] The review covers the maintenance management system export for the depot. The record count and the exact period are described in the appendix.

[P05] ## Findings

[P06] Corrective work was 38.6% of completed work orders [R1]. Mean time to restore on corrective work was 5.4 hours [R2].

[P07] Fleet availability was 96.2% [R3], below the 97.0% target [R4].

[P08] Three assets (TC-07, TC-11 and BR-02) account for 41% of corrective hours [R5].

[P09] The average first-time-fix rate across the four crews was 83.5%, taken as the mean of each crew's rate.

[P10] Spare-part stock-outs delayed 27 work orders [R7].

[P11] ## Risks

[P12] Traction converter TC-07 may fail before its overhaul: likelihood high, impact a three-day withdrawal from service.

[P13] The brake pad interval may be too long for the summer timetable.

[P14] ## Recommendations

[P15] Start a condition-monitoring trial on the three traction converters. Owner: depot engineering manager. Date: end of the next quarter. Serves the objective of 97.0% fleet availability in the corporate plan.

[P16] Review the brake pad change interval against the summer duty cycle. Owner: rolling stock engineer. Date: before the summer timetable.

[P17] Audit the stores for the twelve parts behind the stock-outs. Serves the objective of 97.0% fleet availability.

[P18] The maintenance spend for the year was AED 4.9 million, of which overhaul was AED 1.2 million [R8].
