| Use case | Transferable method | Required RMD inputs | Useful output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly report review | Defined grain, evidence checks, independent KPI calculation | Submitted report/workbook, approved KPI dictionary, period and exclusions | Reported-versus-recalculated results and source-linked exceptions |

| Knowledge and learning | Scoped explanations and source references | Authorised documents and the relevant user role | Answers that retain uncertainty and do not confuse examples with policy |

These are proposed applications. Do not claim implementation, operational benefit or organisational approval solely because they appear in this file.

### 19.1 Example of a safe monthly KPI answer

~~~text
Finding:
The reported KPI and the independently calculated result do not match.

Evidence:
Identify the report version, workbook sheet/cells, reporting period and the
approved formula used.

Reason:
State the verified cause, or say it remains unresolved. Do not invent one.

Decision support:
Explain the effect on the management conclusion and list the missing input
or clarification needed.

Status:
Draft review observation, pending the responsible reviewer's decision.
~~~

### 19.2 Distinguish four different kinds of output

| Output | What it means |
|---|---|
| Historical analysis | Describes patterns already observed |
| Prediction | Estimates a future outcome using information available before it occurs |
| Recommendation | Proposes an action given evidence, objectives and constraints |
| Authorised execution | Performs an action under the application's actual permissions and workflow |

Evidence for one does not establish the others.
