### 05.1 Individual Power BI assignment

Source: S03, both pages. These are requirements from the inspected brief, not inferred expectations.

- Choose a suitable structured business dataset from Kaggle.
- Obtain instructor approval for the dataset title/link, one-sentence business question and suitability explanation.
- Identify the business problem, management decision, variable meanings and data limitations.
- Import into Power BI, perform justified preparation and document the main transformations.
- Build a focused management dashboard using useful KPIs and visuals.
- Use filters or slicers when they improve analysis.
- Apply at least one appropriate decision-support feature, such as Key Influencers, Decomposition Tree or What-If analysis.
- Explain evidence-supported findings in business language.
- Recommend a specific management action and a KPI to monitor after implementation.
- Submit the student's own PBIX plus a concise professional report, dataset reference and necessary screenshots.
- Be able to explain and reproduce the work individually.
- Respect the explicit restrictions on AI-generated assessed analysis and recommendations.

The existing report is recorded in §15 as an existing artifact. This handover does not certify authorship, instructor approval, originality or a final grade.

### 05.2 Group project

Source: S04, both pages.

The group acts as a consulting team. The central decision is what an organisation should implement, why that solution and partner are justified, and how the change will create measurable business value.

Required components:

| Component | Required substance |
|---|---|
| A. Business use case | A realistic organisation/process, one clear problem or opportunity, and a management decision |
| B. Evidence | A manageable primary method or another instructor-approved method; analysed findings |
| C. Gap analysis | As-Is, To-Be, gaps in process/data/technology/people/governance, and requirements |
| D. Solution and partner | Realistic alternatives, explicit criteria, decision matrix and justified selection |
| E. Business strategy | Strategic contribution, expected value, KPIs and management recommendation |
| F. Implementation | Stakeholders, ownership, readiness, phased approach, training, resistance, integration, risks and decision milestones |
| G. Zapier demonstration | A useful workflow with email, an AI/agent step where appropriate, safe sample data and execution evidence |

Required report content includes a cover, contents, use case, research/evidence, gaps, solution/partner comparison, strategy/value/KPIs, roadmap, Zapier evidence, recommendation, references and appendices. The role and contribution sheet is mandatory.

The official brief describes five lead roles. The G02 team added a sixth integration and QA role. That is the team's arrangement, not a claim that the professor prescribed six roles. [S04; S05, slides 1, 27]

Each member must understand the complete proposal and present their own contribution. The instructor may adjust individual marks when contribution evidence is substantially unequal. [S04, p. 2]
