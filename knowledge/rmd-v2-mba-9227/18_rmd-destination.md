### 18.1 User-requested product direction

Source: S10, historical conversation context. These are requested capabilities and preferences, not verified current implementation.

- Executive workspace for railway maintenance management.
- Central Jarvis-style assistant for questions about cases, actions, evidence and displayed KPIs.
- Arabic and English text/voice interaction, with clear bilingual terminology.
- Push-to-talk, transcription, interruption/resumption and voice output were requested.
- Answers linked to source evidence, the relevant case and the current analytical context.
- Visually strong executive dashboards that remain usable and readable.
- Clear distinction between questions, proposed actions and actions actually executed.
- Exportable executive reports and presentations; user interest also includes videos.
- Actual enterprise operation and complete workflows, rather than describing a demo as the live platform.

No assumption is made here about the current frontend framework, backend, hosting, database, authentication provider, vector store, voice provider or approved enterprise connector.

### 18.3 Operational routing lessons retained at principle level

Earlier RMD V2 work distinguished technical maintenance investigation from operational incident leadership, and required checking asset existence and scope before routing. It also distinguished acknowledgement, technical assessment, customer update and repair clocks. [S10]

The proposed CRM routes in that work were not automatically official approved responsibilities. This knowledge pack deliberately does not convert proposed routes into binding duties or invent SLA durations.
