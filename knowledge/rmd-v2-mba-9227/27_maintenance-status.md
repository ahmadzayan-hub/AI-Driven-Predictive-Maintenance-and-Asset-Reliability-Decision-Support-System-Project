## 27. Maintenance and handover status
<!-- kb: domain=meta.maintenance -->


### 27.1 Update procedure

When new evidence arrives:

1. Add its source ID, version, date, locator and domain.
2. Identify which facts it confirms, supersedes or conflicts with.
3. Update affected records and the Course Control Sheet where applicable.
4. Preserve prior versions and explain the reason for a material correction.
5. Re-run only the relevant retrieval/behaviour cases.
6. Record actual verification separately from documentary claims.
