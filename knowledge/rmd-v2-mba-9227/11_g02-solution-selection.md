## 11. Solution selection and partner model
<!-- kb: domain=mba.9227.g02; limits=academic_case|not_rmd_operational_rule -->


Status: historical academic evaluation reported by S05, slides 18 to 20. Vendor prices, capabilities, partner availability and legal terms were not reverified as current market facts.

### 11.1 Alternatives

The team compared a manual process, low-code automation, helpdesk automation, Microsoft automation, delivery tracking and a custom build.

The deck's historical indicative prices included:

- Zapier Team: US$69/month for 2,000 tasks on annual billing.
- Zendesk Suite Professional plus Copilot: US$165 per agent/month, comprising US$115 plus US$50.
- Power Automate Premium: US$15 per user/month on annual billing.
- AfterShip: an estimated approximately US$9,600/year at approximately 80,000 shipments.
- Manual process and custom build: staff or development cost, without a complete estimate.

These figures describe the September 2026 project comparison only. Do not use them for a new procurement budget, current recommendation or RTA licence entitlement decision.

### 11.2 Decision matrix

Criteria weights: functionality 20%; integration 15%; security/privacy/governance 15%; total cost 15%; usability/change 10%; scalability/reliability 10%; partner/local fit 5%; strategic fit 10%.

Scores range from 1 to 5. The weighted sum arithmetic was recomputed from the deck and matches the totals below. The scores themselves remain team judgements from dated vendor material, not independently observed product trial results.

| Alternative | Function 20 | Integration 15 | Security 15 | Cost 15 | Usability 10 | Scale 10 | Partner 5 | Strategic 10 | Weighted /5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Zendesk Professional + Copilot | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3.55 |
| AfterShip | 3 | 5 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 3.40 |
| Power Automate + Power BI | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3.30 |
| Zapier | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3.15 |
| Custom build | 3 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 2.80 |
| Process-only baseline | 1 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 2.30 |

The presentation labels AfterShip conditional because Portuguese templates and LGPD terms had not been confirmed. A score does not remove a failed or unresolved mandatory criterion.

The presentation reports that cost-weight sensitivity can change the preferred alternative: with cost at 30%, Power Automate leads Zendesk. Preserve the decision lesson that a ranking depends on assumptions and weights.

### 11.3 Selected partner approach

The proposal uses a hybrid model: a certified Brazilian Zendesk implementation partner configures the pilot and trains the team, then hands capability to internal staff. The deck suggests an RFP to two or three candidates and mentions GNX Tech and BCR.CX as examples. [S05, slide 20]

These are historical candidate examples, not appointed partners, current endorsements or RMD suppliers.

Phase 2 carrier tracking is conditional on measured detection limits and satisfactory due diligence. The workflow design from this case can inform RMD thinking; the vendor choice does not transfer automatically.
