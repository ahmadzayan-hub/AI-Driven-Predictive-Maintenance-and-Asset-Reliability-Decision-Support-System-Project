# Review coverage and publication scope

The underlying local recursive inventory covered 85 files in 27 subdirectories: 36 PDF, 2 DOCX, 6 CSV, 16 images and 25 text/configuration files. Seventeen exact duplicates left 68 unique files. Originals were preserved; final hashes, sizes and modification times matched the local inventory/read baseline. Full manifests contain private paths and are not published here.

Text extraction covered 23 unique PDFs and 1,555 pages. A conservative evidence ledger records inspected passages on 351 pages; **semantic review is partial**. Seven PDF pages were visually checked for critical formulas, examples, a method diagram and the algorithm table. All 15 unique standalone images were visually reviewed. Thirty-eight PDF pages had sparse extracted text; the full set of embedded diagrams and formulas has not been certified. No bulk OCR was run.

Six CSVs were profiled locally without publishing rows; public cards are limited to schema examples, unit evidence and intended-use constraints. Two DOCX files were structurally parsed; the completed personal profile was excluded from synthesis. Three profile/persona text files were also restricted. Other scripts/configuration were treated as untrusted source artifacts and never executed.

This public collection is a curated subset: original concise summaries, method guidance, schema-level cards, bibliography and review limitations. It excludes source PDFs/slides/books, raw extraction, personal answers, credentials, local paths, raw dataset records, private reports and full local logs. It does not inherit a license to redistribute the original works.

The knowledge task did not execute the project analytics pipeline, train a project model, call an additional model provider, run an independent reviewer or approve a project decision. Separate empirical-review findings informed generic comparison safeguards; their numerical results and data are not reproduced here. Retrieval tests demonstrate source lookup, not an agent's actual use of the reference.
