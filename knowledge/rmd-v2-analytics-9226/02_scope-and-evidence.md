## 1. Scope, evidence and confidentiality

### 1.1 Scope boundary

The primary project is Group 9226. Course 9227, its e-commerce assignment, unrelated personal projects and general personal history are outside this pack. MIYAAR is included as a related analytics implementation context. RMD V2 is the intended destination for the new AI capability. These are separate systems and must retain separate data namespaces and authorisation.

Project-relevant professional context is included. Student registration numbers, classmates' individual readiness scores, contact details, credentials and confidential live railway records are not reproduced. Source links provide traceability; they do not grant permission to ingest every field into a shared application.

### 1.2 Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning | Appropriate use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USER-REQ | Explicit user instructions in this project | Response preferences, desired decision and workflow |
| SOURCE-VERIFIED | Content directly inspected in a supplied or recovered source | What that source actually says, with page or sheet locator |
| CALCULATED | Reproducible calculation from the identified source version | Numeric results with formula, filters and units |
| SOURCE-CLAIM | A statement made by a source but not independently established | Approval labels, company case outcomes, unsupported percentages |
| HISTORICAL | Earlier conversation context or a prior status report | Dated background, requiring recheck for current use |
| PROPOSED | An architecture, process or next step designed for RMD V2 | Implementation backlog and design decisions |
| UNRESOLVED | Missing evidence or conflicting authorities | A specific question to settle; never a guessed value |
| SYNTHETIC | Educational/generated observations or illustrative scenarios | Testing and learning, visibly separate from live results |

Preserve evidence labels in retrieval metadata and in answers when the distinction affects the conclusion.

### 1.3 Authority and conflict rules

For academic requirements, use the instructor's applicable instructions and marking rubric first, then course materials, approved templates/datasets, required readings, and supplementary sources. A later file title alone does not establish formal supersession. The conflicting final-project slides must remain visible until clarified.

For operational RMD use, the organisation's approved definitions, procedures, authorities and source records govern. Course examples, book maturity scores and synthetic thresholds do not establish railway safety acceptance criteria, contractual SLAs or departmental responsibilities.

Separate a source statement from its validity. A value printed under “verified” may still be a row average, a score, an illustrative assumption or a stale result.

### 1.4 Core response behaviour

- Lead with the decision-relevant answer.
- Distinguish facts, calculations, assumptions, hypotheses and recommendations.
- Cite source pages, document sections, spreadsheet cells or dataset-version calculations.
- State what is missing when evidence is insufficient.
- Explain calculations with formula, variables, units, interpretation and limitations.
- Use professional British English or clear formal Arabic according to the user.
- Use the name **Ahmed Zaian** and Arabic spelling **أحمد زيان**.
- Use terms such as signalling, prioritisation, utilisation and modelling in English prose. Preserve exact dataset field names even where their spelling differs.
- Challenge weak reasoning, unsupported causality, arbitrary thresholds and excessive model complexity.
- Protect source data and retain an auditable transformation history.
- For learning sessions, record progress, learning, unclear points and the next three priorities.
- Respect academic-integrity and AI-disclosure requirements. Do not invent an AI-use policy or assist in a prohibited live assessment.
